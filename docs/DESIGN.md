# AgentFoundry Design System

## Brand idea

**Forge your local intelligence.**

AgentFoundry combines modern local-AI tooling with restrained Greco-Roman mythic symbolism. The product should feel powerful, calm, precise and premium — never like a fantasy game or a generic neon AI dashboard.

## Visual principle

> Mythology creates atmosphere. The interface creates trust.

Use classical references as accents: laurel forms, marble surfaces, restrained gold ornament, geometric statues and names inspired by Roman/Greek archetypes. Keep controls, settings and logs highly legible.

## Typography

- Brand / hero: classical serif direction (Georgia fallback; later Cinzel/Cormorant-compatible licensed assets may be evaluated separately)
- UI: Segoe UI / Inter-like sans serif
- Logs: Cascadia Mono / JetBrains Mono direction

Do not bundle third-party font files in the repository without verifying redistribution rights.

## Mythic functional names

Names are secondary labels, never replacements for clear functionality.

- **Minerva** — models, intelligence, model profiles
- **Vulcan** — runtime, inference engine, hardware tuning
- **Jupiter** — overall system/orchestration state
- **Hermes** — agents, tool calling, connections
- **Apollo** — benchmarks and performance

## Artwork

Promotional artwork may include male and female digitized classical deities, subtle circuitry, holographic geometry, temple architecture and celestial motifs.

For the desktop application itself, artwork should be understated. Prefer small reliefs, silhouettes, geometric symbols or low-contrast backgrounds over large character art.

## UI rules

1. Gold is a scarce brand color; use it for primary actions and identity, not every control.
2. Green is reserved for healthy/running state.
3. Blue/violet represent compute, model and network activity.
4. Logs and technical settings remain visually neutral and highly readable.
5. Mythic decoration must never obscure model paths, runtime parameters or errors.
6. The app should still make sense with all decorative artwork removed.

## Logo direction

The current mark combines a laurel wreath with a geometric forge flame and an AI node. It must remain recognizable at small icon sizes and work in monochrome.

## Voice

AgentFoundry should sound technical, calm and capable.

Preferred phrases:

- Forge your local intelligence.
- Modern tools. Ancient wisdom.
- Your models. Your tools. Your machine.

Avoid grandiose claims about intelligence, consciousness or autonomy that the software cannot substantiate.
